Rooted in Tradition

A family-owned coffee shop and gift store serving Washington's community with warmth and authenticity since our founding.

At Atticus Coffee & Gifts, we believe that great coffee brings people together. Our carefully sourced beans are roasted with passion, creating blends that tell stories of their origins. Beyond exceptional coffee, we curate unique handmade gifts from local artisans, making us your destination for both daily comfort and special moments.
